# Elizabeth's Guide: Bi-Weekly Event Collection & Upcoming Events Digest

**Target Audience:** Elizabeth (Event Coordinator / Administrator)  
**Organization:** Cork City Partnership CLG  
**System:** CCP Event Calendar  
**Live Site:** `https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/`  
**Direct Submission Link:** `https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/submit`  
**Cadence:** Bi-weekly (Wednesday call-out → Thursday review → Friday circulation)

---

## 🌟 Overview & Purpose

Following the management directive from Brenda, Cork City Partnership operates an internal workflow to ensure **upcoming events** receive dedicated visibility:

> *"While we use our social media and WhatsApp very successfully for events that have already taken place, upcoming events are easily lost in the volume of messages. It is essential that the Board of Directors and all staff are notified separately of forthcoming events two weeks in advance, so everyone can pencil them into their diaries."*

As the coordinator, your role is supported by the **CCP Event Calendar**, which streamlines the entire process:
1. Staff submit their events via a dedicated, password-free form.
2. You review, edit, and approve them in your Admin Submissions Inbox.
3. You generate a professional branded **PDF Upcoming Events Digest** and a 1-click **WhatsApp Digest** with the push of a button.

## Step 1: Wednesday Afternoon — Send Call-Out Email

Every Wednesday afternoon, send an email to all CCP staff reminding them to submit any events taking place over the **following two weeks**.

### Direct Link to Share:
> **Submission URL:** `https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/submit`  
> 
> *Staff do not need to log in or remember passwords. The form opens directly on any computer, tablet, or smartphone. It can also be accessed directly on the login page ([https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/](https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/)) right under the Sign In button.*

### Copy-Paste Email Template for Wednesday:

```text
Subject: Upcoming Events Call-Out: Next 2 Weeks [Submissions Due Thursday 5:00 PM]

Hi everyone,

A reminder to please submit details for any upcoming events, activities, or project milestones scheduled for the next two weeks. 

These will be compiled into our bi-weekly Upcoming Events Digest and circulated to the Board of Directors and all staff this Friday morning.

👉 Submit your event here (takes 2 minutes, no login needed):
https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/submit
(Or visit https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/ and click "Submit an Event for review" below the Sign In button)

What kind of events?
• Enterprise network meetings & business clinics
• Community gatherings & family fun days in estates
• Lord Mayor or dignitary visits to projects
• Certificate presentations & course graduations
• Public information sessions, open days & workshops
• Launches, celebrations, or community milestone events

Information needed:
1. Name of the event
2. Date & start/finish time
3. Venue and location (room/building/address)
4. A few lines describing what the event is about
5. Optional: Event flyer or poster image (PNG/JPG)

Please submit all items by Thursday 5:00 PM so they can be included in Friday's release.

Thank you for your cooperation!

Best regards,
Elizabeth
Cork City Partnership CLG
```

## Step 2: Thursday — Review & Moderate Submissions

When staff submit events, they arrive directly in your **Submissions Inbox** with status `Pending Review`.

### How to Moderate:

1. **Log in to the Calendar:**
   * Go to `https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/` and sign in with your Admin email and password.

2. **Check the Submissions Badge:**
   * Look at the top navigation bar. If there are pending submissions, you will see a badge:
   * `[📥 Submissions (3)]` *(the badge pulses when new items are waiting)*.

3. **Open the Inbox:**
   * Click the **"Submissions"** button to open the moderation drawer.

4. **Review Each Submission:**
   * Each card displays:
     * **Event title & category** (e.g., *Community & Family*, *Enterprise & Employment*).
     * **Date & time**.
     * **Venue / location**.
     * **Summary description**.
     * **Submitter's name & email** (e.g., *Submitted by Sarah Murphy (sarah@partnershipcork.ie)*).
     * **Flyer preview** (click the thumbnail to view the full image).

5. **Take Action:**
   * **Approve & Publish (Green Checkmark):** Click to publish immediately. The event instantly appears on the live calendar and is marked ready for Friday's digest.
   * **Edit (Pencil Icon):** Need to fix a typo, clarify the venue, or refine the time? Click **Edit** to modify the event details, then save.
   * **Reject / Delete (Trash Icon):** If a submission is a duplicate or spam, click **Reject** to remove it.
   * **Approve All:** If you have verified all entries at once, click **"Approve All"** in the top right of the modal.

## Step 3: Friday Morning — Generate the Upcoming Events Digest

Once submissions are approved, you can generate both the **branded PDF** and the **WhatsApp digest** in under 60 seconds.

### 1. Open the Generator
* In the top navigation bar, click the **"Events Digest"** button (with the document icon).
* *(Alternatively, click "Export" and choose "Upcoming Events Digest").*

### 2. Check the Date Range
* The modal automatically sets the range to **the next 14 days** (today through +14 days).
* You can adjust the start and end dates if you want to include events further out.

### 3. Choose the Layout Style
* **Executive Digest (Recommended for Board of Directors):**
  * Branded A4 document with Cork City Partnership logo, magenta (`#B30066`) and green (`#39B54A`) styling.
  * Individual event cards with date badges, venue map links, descriptions, and embedded flyer thumbnails.
  * Built-in interactive buttons allowing Board members to add events to **Outlook 365** or download an **.ics** file in 1 click.
* **Compact Table:**
  * Clean, dense agenda table suitable for printing as a one-page handout.

### 4. Download the PDF
* Click **"Download Events Digest PDF"**.
* The file will download to your computer as:
  `CCP-Events-Digest-YYYY-MM-DD.pdf`

### 5. Copy the WhatsApp Digest
* Click **"Copy Formatted WhatsApp Summary"**.
* A green toast notification will appear: `Copied WhatsApp Text to Clipboard!`.
* You can now paste (`Ctrl + V`) directly into company WhatsApp groups. The text includes bold titles, dates, times, Google Maps venue links, and descriptions.

## 💡 Frequently Asked Questions & Pro-Tips

### What if a staff member emails me their event instead of using the form?
You don't need to type it into an email or re-route them. You have two options:
1. Open `https://ccp-event-calendar.pages.dev/submit` and fill it out on their behalf (since you are logged in as admin, it will automatically publish!).
2. In the calendar view, click the **"+ New Event"** button in the bottom corner and enter the details.

### What if someone submits an event on Friday morning after I already generated the PDF?
You can simply approve the submission in the **Submissions Inbox**, then click **"Events Digest"** and click **Download** again. The new PDF will immediately include the latest event.

### What if an event date or venue changes after publication?
1. Open the main calendar view.
2. Click on the event card.
3. Click the **Edit (Pencil)** icon.
4. Update the date, time, or location, and click **Save**.
5. The live calendar and any newly generated digest will reflect the update instantly.

### Can staff view the submissions inbox?
No. The **Submissions** button and inbox are strictly restricted to administrators (Elizabeth and designated admins). Staff only see published events on the public calendar.
